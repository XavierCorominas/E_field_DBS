**Deep Brain Stimulation Electrode Monitoring of TMS/tCS/tES-Induced Electric Fields**

This repository contains code for estimating the impact of electric fields generated by any transcranial electric or magnetic stimulation (tES, TMS), or ultrasound (FUS), on implanted deep brain stimulation (DBS) electrodes in subject-specific space.

Accurately estimating the electric field or acoustic pressure at the electrode contacts is essential to ensure safety and to avoid biased recordings. 
This prevents misinterpreting direct polarization of the electrodes by exogenous fields as neuronal responses.
